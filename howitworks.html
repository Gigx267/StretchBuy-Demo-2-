Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which stores are available in my area?

When you enter your location, we automatically show you all participating stores in your delivery area. You can browse by store or search for specific items across all local options.

What if an item I want is out of stock?

Our system shows real-time inventory from stores. If an item becomes unavailable after you've added it to your cart, we'll notify you immediately and suggest similar alternatives.

Can I communicate with the shopper during my order?

Yes! Once your order is being shopped, you can chat directly with your shopper through our app to provide special instructions or approve substitutions.

How are delivery fees calculated?

Delivery fees vary based on distance from the store and your selected delivery speed. Fees are clearly displayed before checkout. Many stores also offer free delivery for orders over a certain amount.

What if I'm not satisfied with my order?

We guarantee your satisfaction. If any items don't meet your expectations, simply report the issue in the app within 24 hours and we'll issue a refund or credit.
